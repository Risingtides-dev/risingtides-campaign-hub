# 4c. Active vs. Finished Tabs

**Location:** On the Campaigns List (homepage), just above the campaigns table.

---

## What they are

The campaigns list is split into two tabs to keep the working view clean:

- **Active** — campaigns that are still running (anything not yet fully wrapped)
- **Finished** — campaigns that have been marked as completed (green checkmark)

---

## How a campaign moves to Finished

On the Campaigns List, every row has a small box in the far left column. Clicking that box cycles through three states:

| State | What it looks like | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **Empty box** | Plain white square | Campaign is in progress — nothing has been formally marked |
| **Gray checkmark** | Gray ✓ in a gray box | Booking is complete — all creators are locked in |
| **Green checkmark** | Green ✓ in a green box | Campaign is fully wrapped — posts delivered, payments done |

Once a campaign has a **green checkmark**, it automatically moves from the **Active** tab to the **Finished** tab. It doesn't get deleted — it's just archived out of the daily working view.

Clicking the green checkmark a third time resets it back to empty, moving the campaign back to Active. This is useful if it was marked done by mistake.

---

## Why this matters

On a busy week, the team might have 15+ active campaigns. The Finished tab keeps wrapped campaigns accessible (for reference, payments, or disputes) without cluttering the main view.

The search bar works on whichever tab you're currently looking at — so you can search within Finished campaigns too.

---

## Quick reference

| I want to... | Do this |
|---|---|
| See what's currently running | Click the Active tab |
| See past campaigns | Click the Finished tab |
| Mark a campaign as done | Click the checkbox once (gray ✓) then again (green ✓) |
| Undo a "finished" mark | Click the green ✓ again to reset it to empty |
