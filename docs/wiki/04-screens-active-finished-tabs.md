# 4c. Active vs. Finished Tabs

**Location:** On the Promotions page (homepage), just above the campaigns table.

---

## What they are

The campaigns list is split into two tabs to keep the working view clean:

- **Active** — campaigns that are still running (anything that hasn't been fully wrapped)
- **Finished** — campaigns that have been marked as completed (green checkmark)

Each tab shows a count in parentheses, e.g. "Active (12)" and "Finished (3)."

---

## How a campaign moves to Finished

Every row in the Promotions table has a small box in the far-left column. Clicking it cycles through three states:

| State | What it looks like | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **Empty box** | Plain white square | In progress — nothing has been formally marked |
| **Gray checkmark** | Gray ✓ in a gray box | Booking is complete — all creators are locked in |
| **Green checkmark** | Green ✓ in a green box | Campaign is fully wrapped — posts delivered, payments done |

Once a campaign has the **green checkmark**, it automatically moves from the **Active** tab to the **Finished** tab. It doesn't get deleted — it's just archived out of the daily working view.

Clicking the green checkmark a third time resets the campaign back to empty, moving it back to Active. Useful if it was marked done by mistake.

---

## Why this matters

On a busy week the team might have 15+ active campaigns. The Finished tab keeps wrapped campaigns accessible (for reference, late payments, or disputes) without cluttering the main view.

The search bar works on whichever tab you're currently looking at — so you can search within Finished campaigns too.

---

## Quick reference

| I want to... | Do this |
|---|---|
| See what's currently running | Click the Active tab |
| See past campaigns | Click the Finished tab |
| Mark a campaign as done | Click the checkbox once (gray ✓), then again (green ✓) |
| Undo a "finished" mark | Click the green ✓ once more to reset to empty |
