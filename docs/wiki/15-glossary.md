# 15. Glossary

Plain-English definitions for every term you'll encounter in Campaign Hub.

---

**Active campaign** — A campaign that is currently running. Appears on the Active tab of the Promotions page. Becomes "finished" when you mark it complete with a green checkmark.

**Artist** — The musician whose song is being promoted in a campaign.

**Booking** — The act of hiring a creator for a campaign. A booking specifies the creator's username, how many posts they'll make, and how much they'll be paid.

**Budget** — The total amount of money allocated for paying creators on a campaign. Set when the campaign is created and tracked against actual payments.

**Budget Used %** — The percentage of the campaign budget that has been paid out to creators so far. Calculated automatically.

**Campaign** — A single music promotion effort. Tied to one song and one label. Has a budget, a roster of creators, and a set of posts to track.

**Campaign Hub** — This app. Rising Tides' internal tool for managing all of the above.

**Campaign slug** — A short, URL-safe version of the campaign name, like `sombr-homewrecker-promo-r3`. Used in web addresses and as a unique identifier inside the system.

**Cobrand** — The third-party platform Rising Tides uses to track how well campaign posts are performing. Measures views, engagement, and submission counts. Performance data lives in Cobrand, not in Campaign Hub.

**Cobrand share page** — A specific URL on Cobrand's website that shows the live stats for a campaign. You paste this link into Campaign Hub to connect the two.

**Cobrand upload page** — The part of Cobrand where you paste post links so Cobrand can start tracking them. Campaign Hub embeds this directly in the campaign detail page.

**Completion status** — The stage of a campaign's lifecycle. Three states: none (active), booked (creators locked in), completed (finished, green check).

**CPM** — "Cost per mille" — cost per thousand views. Calculated as: (total paid out ÷ total views) × 1,000. A lower CPM means you got more views per dollar spent.

**Creator** — A TikTok or Instagram account owner who is paid to post videos using a specific song. Also called an influencer or content creator.

**Creator database** — The cross-campaign roster page showing all creators who have ever been booked on any campaign.

**Finished campaign** — A campaign marked as completed (green checkmark). Appears on the Finished tab of the Promotions page. Still viewable and editable; just removed from the active workflow.

**Internal creators** — TikTok accounts monitored by the Internal TikTok tool — Rising Tides team accounts and label partner pages. Not to be confused with external campaign creators.

**Label** — A record label (e.g., Warner Bros., Atlantic Records) that books Rising Tides to run promotion campaigns. The label pays Rising Tides; Rising Tides pays creators.

**Matched video** — A creator's TikTok or Instagram post that the scraper confirmed uses the campaign's sound. Counted in "Posts Matched."

**Notion** — The tool Rising Tides uses to manage client relationships. Client info and label bookings live in Notion. New "Client" entries in Notion automatically create campaigns in Campaign Hub.

**Notion CRM** — The Notion database that stores client and campaign booking information. CRM stands for "client relationship management."

**Open CLAW** — The AI assistant that reads Rising Tides' Slack booking channel and sends parsed booking recommendations to Campaign Hub's Slack Inbox.

**Outreach Hub** — The Campaign Hub section for managing DM-based outreach to creators in the network. Used to send booking offers and track responses.

**PayPal memory** — Campaign Hub automatically remembers a creator's PayPal email address from previous campaigns. When you add that creator again, their PayPal fills in automatically.

**Platform** — The social media network a creator posts on. In Campaign Hub: TikTok or Instagram.

**Platform split** — When a campaign runs on both TikTok and Instagram, the platform split records what percentage of the budget goes to each platform.

**Posts Done** — How many posts a creator has delivered (as counted by the scraper finding matched videos).

**Posts Matched** — How many of a creator's posts were confirmed to use the campaign's sound. Usually the same as Posts Done, but may differ if a creator posted extra videos.

**Posts Owed** — How many posts a creator agreed to make as part of their booking.

**Project lead** — The Rising Tides team member responsible for managing a campaign. Recorded in Campaign Hub but owned as a concept by Notion.

**Promotions** — The name used in Campaign Hub for the campaigns list homepage.

**Round** — Some campaigns are run in multiple rounds (e.g., Round 1, Round 2, Round 3). The round number tracks which phase of the campaign this is.

**Scraping** — The automated process of visiting TikTok or Instagram to find posts that use a campaign's sound. See [The Scrapers](13-scrapers.md) for a full explanation.

**Slack Inbox** — The Campaign Hub page where booking recommendations sent by Open CLAW appear for Jake to approve or dismiss.

**Slug** — A short, URL-safe identifier. See "Campaign slug" above.

**Sound / Sound ID** — Every sound on TikTok has a unique numeric ID. When a creator makes a video using a specific song, that video is tagged with the sound ID. Campaign Hub uses this ID to find matching posts via the scraper.

**TidesTrackers** — Campaign Hub's name for Cobrand tracking links. Accessible in the Tracking section of the sidebar.

**UGC** — User-generated content. Videos made by real people (creators) rather than professionally produced advertisements. The type of content Rising Tides campaigns are built around.
