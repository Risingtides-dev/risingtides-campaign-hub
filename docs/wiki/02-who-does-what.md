# 2. Who Does What

## Jake

Jake is the founder and main operator of Rising Tides. He runs the campaigns, makes the final call on which creators get booked, and approves all incoming booking suggestions. When something breaks or looks wrong, Jake is the person to ask.

Campaign Hub was originally Jake's personal tool — he built the first version himself. Everything you see in it reflects how Jake thinks about running campaigns.

## The team

The Rising Tides team is small. Different people manage different TikTok and Instagram pages that are used internally (you'll see names like Jake, John, Sam, Eric, Johnny, and Seeno in the Internal TikTok section). The team also works with label contacts at Warner and Atlantic.

## Creators

Creators are the TikTok and Instagram content makers who get paid to post videos using a specific song.

Here's how it works: Rising Tides reaches out to (or is approached by) creators who have audiences that fit the vibe of a particular song. Rising Tides agrees on a rate with each creator — say, $150 for five posts. The creator posts the videos on their own account, using the song. Once the posts go live, Rising Tides logs them and eventually pays out.

Creators don't work for Rising Tides full-time. They're freelancers who work campaign by campaign. The same creator might show up across many different campaigns over time — that's why Campaign Hub has a "Creator Database" that tracks each creator's full history across every campaign they've been a part of.

## Labels / Clients

Labels are the record companies that hire Rising Tides. In practice, this usually means Warner Music Group, Atlantic Records, and similar major and mid-size labels.

The label typically comes to Rising Tides with a song they want promoted and a budget they're willing to spend. Rising Tides takes that budget, allocates it across creators, runs the campaign, and delivers the results.

Campaign Hub tracks the campaign budget and creator payments — but the label's own billing (what they owe Rising Tides, invoices, contracts) lives in Notion, not here.

## What is UGC?

UGC stands for **User-Generated Content**. In plain English: real people making real videos, not a brand producing polished ads.

When a song is used in a TikTok video by someone who genuinely seems to like it, that feels authentic. When millions of people see that kind of content, it drives real streams, playlist adds, and cultural buzz. Labels pay for UGC campaigns because that organic-looking content works better than traditional ads for the age group that uses TikTok.

Rising Tides' job is to find the right creators, get them posting with the right song, and make sure those posts happen. The fact that creators are being paid is disclosed by the creators themselves in their posts (as required by FTC rules) — but the content itself is genuine in the sense that the creators pick their own creative angle.
