# 2. Who Does What

## The Rising Tides Team

**Jake** is the founder and runs most things operationally. He decides which creators to book, approves incoming booking suggestions, checks campaign performance, and handles creator payments. Campaign Hub was originally built by Jake and is designed around how he works.

The broader team includes account managers and operations staff who use Campaign Hub to check campaign status, look up creator history, and keep records up to date.

## The Labels (Clients)

Labels are Rising Tides' paying clients — companies like Warner Bros. Records, Atlantic Records, and similar music labels. A label comes to Rising Tides and says: "We want to promote this song. We're paying you to run a campaign on TikTok and Instagram." That booking gets recorded in Notion (a separate tool) and then flows into Campaign Hub as a new campaign.

Campaign Hub tracks what Rising Tides owes the label in terms of results, but the billing relationship (what the label actually pays Rising Tides) stays in Notion. That's not Campaign Hub's job.

## The Creators

Creators are the TikTok and Instagram accounts that Rising Tides hires to post videos using a promoted song. They might have a few thousand followers or a few million. They get paid per post — the rate and number of posts owed are tracked for each creator per campaign.

When a creator is booked for a campaign, their username, agreed rate, number of posts they owe, and PayPal email are all recorded in Campaign Hub. Once they post, the scraper finds their videos and links them to the campaign.

## What Is UGC?

UGC stands for "user-generated content." It means real people making real videos, as opposed to polished advertisements. A label wants creators to use their song in organic-looking TikTok videos — not TV commercials. The idea is that a song used by many real creators looks like it's naturally trending, which drives streams and chart performance.

Rising Tides specializes in running UGC campaigns. Campaign Hub is the tool that makes those campaigns manageable at scale — multiple campaigns running at once, each with dozens of creators, each with posts to track and payments to make.
