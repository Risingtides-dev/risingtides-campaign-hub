# 7. The Creator Profile Page

**Where:** Click a creator's username anywhere in the app — from the Creator Database, from a campaign's creator table, or from the Slack Inbox. The web address looks like `/creators/xyzbca_quote`.

**Who uses it:** Jake when deciding whether to re-book someone, or when looking up payment details.

---

## What You See

### Header

At the top: the creator's username, their platform (TikTok or Instagram), and their PayPal email if one is on file.

Below the username are aggregate stats pulled across all their campaigns:

| Stat | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Campaigns** | How many different campaigns they've been part of |
| **Posts Owed** | Total posts they were contracted to deliver |
| **Posts Done** | Total posts the scraper found and confirmed |
| **Total Earned** | Sum of agreed rates across all campaigns |
| **Total Payout** | Sum of amounts actually paid out |
| **Total Views** | Combined views across all their matched videos |
| **Avg CPM** | Their average cost per thousand views |

---

### Campaign History Table

Below the stats is a table of every campaign this creator has been on. Each row is one campaign.

**Columns:**

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Campaign** | Campaign title, artist, and song |
| **Posts Owed** | How many posts they were contracted for on this campaign |
| **Posts Done** | How many were found |
| **Rate** | What they were paid (or are owed) for this campaign |
| **Paid** | Whether payment has been made, and if so when |
| **Status** | Any status notes |
| **Notes** | Per-campaign notes |

Click a campaign row to go to that campaign's detail page.

---

### Matched Videos

Below the campaign table is a list of the actual videos we found from this creator — across all campaigns. Each row shows the video link, which campaign it belongs to, view count, likes, and upload date.

---

## What It's Good For

- **Re-booking decisions** — see all their numbers in one place before reaching out again
- **Payment disputes** — check which campaigns are marked paid vs. unpaid
- **Performance review** — see their view counts and CPM trend across multiple campaigns
