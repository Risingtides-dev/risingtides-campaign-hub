# 4. The Campaigns List (Promotions Page)

**Where:** This is the homepage. You see it when you first open Campaign Hub or click "Promotions" in the sidebar.

**Who uses it:** Everyone on the team. It's the starting point for most daily work.

---

## What You See

The page has a title ("Promotions") and a blue **New Campaign** button in the top right corner.

Below that is a tab bar and search box, followed by the campaign table.

---

## Active and Finished Tabs

The table is split into two tabs:

- **Active** — campaigns that are currently running (everything without a green completion checkmark)
- **Finished** — campaigns that have been marked complete (green checkmark)

The number in parentheses next to each tab tells you how many campaigns are in it, e.g. "Active (12)" or "Finished (4)."

Click a tab to switch between views. You start on Active by default.

---

## The Search Bar

There's a search box in the tab bar. Type anything — a campaign name, artist name, or song title — and the table filters down instantly. You don't need to press Enter. Click the "Clear" button or delete your text to show all campaigns again.

The count on the right side of the search bar shows how many campaigns match your current search.

---

## The Campaign Table

Each row in the table is one campaign. Every column can be sorted — click a column header to sort by that column. Click it again to reverse the sort order.

**Columns:**

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Status** | A small checkbox icon that shows where the campaign is in its lifecycle: empty = active, half-filled = booked, green check = completed. Click to cycle through the states. |
| **Campaign** | The campaign title and a smaller artist / song line below it. Click anywhere in the row to open the campaign detail page. |
| **Start Date** | When the campaign started. |
| **Budget** | The total campaign budget, and below it how much has been paid out so far. |
| **Budget Used** | A percentage showing how much of the budget has been paid out. |
| **Live Posts** | How many creator videos have been found and matched to this campaign. |
| **Total Views** | Combined view count across all matched posts. |
| **CPM** | Cost per thousand views — total budget paid divided by views, times 1,000. A lower number is better. |
| **Creators** | How many creators are booked on this campaign. |

---

## Creating a New Campaign

Click the **New Campaign** button. A form appears above the table with fields for:

- **Title** — the campaign name
- **Artist** — the artist's name
- **Song** — the song title
- **Sound ID** — the TikTok sound ID (used by the scraper to find posts)
- **Budget** — total campaign budget in dollars
- **Start Date** — when the campaign begins

Fill in the fields and click Create. The new campaign appears in the Active tab immediately.

---

## Clicking Into a Campaign

Click any row in the table to open that campaign's detail page. You can also use the back button to return here.
