# 9. The Internal TikTok Tool

**Where:** Click "Internal TikTok" in the sidebar under Internal.

**Who uses it:** Jake and the team to monitor Rising Tides' own TikTok accounts, as well as the accounts owned by labels you work with.

---

## What It Is

The Internal TikTok tool is separate from the campaign scraper. Instead of looking for videos using a song, it looks at specific TikTok accounts and reports back what they've been posting recently.

It's used to keep an eye on:
- **Team accounts** — e.g., Jake's personal account, other team members' accounts
- **Label pages** — the TikTok pages run by Warner, Atlantic, and other label partners

This is useful for spotting trends, checking what sounds labels are pushing, and keeping tabs on what's performing well in your network.

---

## The Main Page Layout

The page is organized into two sections:

### Groups

Groups are named collections of TikTok accounts. Each group appears as a card showing:
- The group name
- How many accounts are in it
- Recent stats: total views, total posts, total likes

Clicking a group card opens the Group Detail page (described below).

There are two types of groups:
- **Person groups** — accounts belonging to individual team members (jake_balik, john_smathers, etc.)
- **Label groups** — pages run by labels (warner_pages, atlantic_pages, etc.)

### Add / Remove Accounts

Below the groups there are controls to add new TikTok usernames to the monitoring list, and to remove ones that are no longer needed.

---

## Running a Scrape

At the top of the page there's a **Scrape** section with options for how many days back to look (e.g., last 7 days, last 30 days). Click the scrape button to start a scan. A progress bar shows how far along it is — the scraper checks each account one at a time and updates the display as it goes.

When the scrape finishes, the groups and their stats update with fresh data.

---

## The Group Detail Page

Click any group card to open its detail page. This shows:
- The full list of accounts in the group
- Total views, posts, and likes for the group in the selected time window
- A leaderboard of top-performing accounts
- The top songs (by view count) that accounts in this group have been posting

---

## The Per-Creator Detail Page

Click any creator's username anywhere in the Internal TikTok section to see that individual's page. It shows:
- All the videos that creator posted in the tracked window
- Videos grouped by song — each song gets a collapsible card showing view count, likes, and a list of the individual post links
- A **Copy Links** button on each song card to copy all the post URLs to your clipboard at once (useful for pasting into Cobrand)

---

## What It's Good For

- Spotting which sounds are getting traction on label pages before you decide to book a campaign
- Checking whether your own team's accounts are still active and posting
- Pulling post links for label clients who ask for examples
