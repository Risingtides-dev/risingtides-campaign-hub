# 10. Sidebar and Navigation

**What it is:** The left-hand navigation panel that lets you jump between the different sections of Campaign Hub.

---

## Desktop Layout

On a laptop or desktop, the sidebar is always visible on the left side of the screen. It's 220 pixels wide and doesn't move as you scroll.

At the very top of the sidebar is the app name: **Campaign Tracker**.

Below that, the navigation links are grouped into labeled sections:

| Section | Links |
|---|---|
| **Campaigns** | Promotions, Scrape Tasks |
| **Creators** | Creator Database |
| **Internal** | Internal TikTok |
| **Outreach** | Outreach Hub |
| **Intake** | Slack Inbox |
| **Tracking** | TidesTrackers |
| **Distribution** | Sound Assignments |

The currently active page is highlighted in blue, with a blue left border. All other links are grey and highlight on hover.

---

## Mobile Layout

On a phone or narrow screen, the sidebar is hidden by default. A hamburger menu button (three horizontal lines) appears in the top-left corner of the screen.

Tap the hamburger to open the sidebar. It slides in from the left as an overlay. A semi-transparent dark backdrop appears behind it — tap anywhere on the backdrop to close the sidebar.

When you tap a navigation link on mobile, the sidebar closes automatically and you go to the new page.

---

## The Sections Explained

**Promotions** — the homepage. Your list of all active and finished campaigns.

**Scrape Tasks** — a workflow view showing campaigns that have unreviewed scraped videos, and the health of the automated scraping system.

**Creator Database** — a cross-campaign roster of every creator you've ever worked with.

**Internal TikTok** — monitoring for your own team's TikTok accounts and label partner pages.

**Outreach Hub** — for managing outreach to creators in your network (sending booking messages, tracking responses).

**Slack Inbox** — incoming booking recommendations from Slack, waiting for approval.

**TidesTrackers** — a list of all your Cobrand tracking links (TidesTrackers), organized into groups and linked to campaigns.

**Sound Assignments** — managing which sounds are assigned to which campaigns, particularly useful when a song has multiple sound IDs across different regions or platforms.
