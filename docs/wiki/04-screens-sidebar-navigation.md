# 4h. Sidebar / Navigation

**Location:** The left side of every page in Campaign Hub.

---

## What it is

The sidebar is the main navigation panel. It's always visible on the left side of the screen on a desktop. On a phone or small tablet, it collapses — you open it by tapping the hamburger menu icon (three horizontal lines) at the top of the screen.

---

---

## Navigation items explained

| Section | Page | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| **Campaigns** | Promotions | The main campaigns table — your daily starting point |
| **Campaigns** | Scrape Tasks | View the status of background scraping jobs |
| **Creators** | Creator Database | Full roster of all creators across all campaigns |
| **Internal** | Internal TikTok | Monitor Rising Tides' own TikTok accounts |
| **Outreach** | Outreach Hub | Manage outreach to potential creators |
| **Intake** | Slack Inbox | Review and approve creator bookings from Slack |
| **Tracking** | TidesTrackers | Internal tracking records linked to campaigns |
| **Distribution** | Sound Assignments | Assign sounds/campaigns to distribution channels |

---

## The active page highlight

The page you're currently on is highlighted in the sidebar — blue background, bold text, and a blue left-border stripe. All other items are plain gray text.

---

## On mobile (phones and small tablets)

On small screens, the sidebar is hidden. A hamburger icon (☰) appears at the top left of the screen. Tap it to slide the sidebar open from the left. A dark overlay covers the rest of the screen. Tap anywhere on that overlay (or on a navigation link) to close the sidebar again.

This means Campaign Hub is fully usable on a phone — the sidebar just gets out of the way when you don't need it.
