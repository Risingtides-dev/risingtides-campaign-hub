# 4h. Sidebar / Navigation

**Location:** The left side of every page in Campaign Hub.

---

## What it is

The sidebar is the main navigation panel. It's always visible on the left side of the screen on a desktop. On a phone or small tablet it collapses — you open it by tapping the hamburger icon (☰) at the top of the screen.

At the very top of the sidebar is the **Campaign Tracker** logo / app name. Below that is a list of navigation sections, each containing one or more links.

---


---

## Every link, explained

| Section | Link | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| **Campaigns** | Promotions | The main campaigns table — your daily starting point |
| **Campaigns** | Scrape Tasks | View the status of background scraping jobs (in-progress, finished, errored) |
| **Creators** | Creator Database | Roster of every creator across every campaign |
| **Internal** | Internal TikTok | Monitor Rising Tides' own TikTok pages and label-partner pages |
| **Outreach** | Outreach Hub | Manage outreach to potential new creators |
| **Intake** | Slack Inbox | Review and approve creator bookings parsed from Slack by Open CLAW |
| **Tracking** | TidesTrackers | Internal tracking records linked to campaigns |
| **Distribution** | Sound Assignments | Assign sounds and campaigns to distribution channels |

---

## Active page highlight

The page you're currently on is highlighted in the sidebar — blue background, bold text, and a blue left-border stripe. Every other link is plain gray text.

---

## On mobile (phones and small tablets)

The sidebar is hidden by default on small screens. A hamburger icon (☰) appears at the top-left. Tap it to slide the sidebar in from the left. A dark overlay covers the rest of the screen — tap anywhere on that overlay (or tap a link) to close the sidebar again.

Campaign Hub is fully usable on a phone. The sidebar just stays out of the way until you need it.
