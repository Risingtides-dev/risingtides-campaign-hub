# 4d. The Creator Database

**URL:** `/creators`

**Who uses it:** Anyone who wants to look up a creator across all campaigns — their history, their total earnings, how many posts they've done.

---

## What it is

The Creator Database is a full roster of every creator who has ever been booked on any campaign. Unlike the Creators Table on a Campaign Detail page (which only shows creators for that one campaign), this page shows everyone across every campaign at once.

Think of it as a contact book for creators, with performance stats attached.

---

## What every column means

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Creator** | The creator's username (with a TikTok icon). Click to open their full Creator Profile. |
| **Campaigns** | How many different campaigns this creator has been booked on |
| **Total Paid** | The sum of all payments made to this creator across all campaigns |
| **Views** | Total views across all their matched videos across all campaigns |
| **CPM** | Their average cost per 1,000 views across all campaigns |

All columns can be sorted — click a column header to sort ascending or descending.

---

## The search bar

Type any part of a username to filter the table instantly. The table updates as you type.

---

## Clicking a creator

Click any creator's row (or username) to open their **Creator Profile** page — a full breakdown of every campaign they've been on, every post, and their complete payment history.

---

## Why this is useful

Before this screen existed, there was no easy way to answer questions like:

- "Has this creator worked with us before?"
- "What rate did we pay them last time?"
- "How many views do they typically get?"

Now all of that is one click away. You can compare CPMs across creators to make better booking decisions on future campaigns.
