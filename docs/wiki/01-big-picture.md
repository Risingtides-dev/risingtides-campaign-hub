# 1. The Big Picture

## What is Campaign Hub?

Campaign Hub is Rising Tides' internal command center for managing music promotion campaigns. When a record label hires Rising Tides to get a song into TikTok and Instagram content, everything that happens after that phone call — booking the creators, tracking the videos, watching the numbers, paying people out — is managed right here.

Think of it as a dashboard that ties together all the moving parts of a campaign in one place, so nothing slips through the cracks.

## What problem does it solve?

Before Campaign Hub existed, Rising Tides managed all of this in spreadsheets, Notion pages, and memory. When you're running one campaign at a time, that works fine. When you're running a dozen at once, things fall apart: you lose track of which creators got paid, you forget to follow up on missing posts, you can't easily compare how different campaigns are performing.

Campaign Hub brings everything into one place:
- Which creators are booked on each campaign, and at what rate
- How many posts each creator owes and how many they've delivered
- Who's been paid and who hasn't
- How much of the budget has been spent and how much is left
- Live numbers from the tracking system (how many views, how many submissions)

The goal is simple: at any moment, Jake or anyone on the team should be able to open Campaign Hub and immediately know exactly where every campaign stands.

## Who pays for the service, and what do they get?

**Record labels** (think Warner, Atlantic, and others) are the clients. They hire Rising Tides to promote their artists' songs. The label pays Rising Tides a fee for the campaign, and in return Rising Tides delivers posts from real content creators on TikTok and/or Instagram.

The creators are the people who actually make the videos. They're paid by Rising Tides out of the campaign budget. The label never talks directly to the creators — that's what Rising Tides handles.

**The end result the label wants:** their song showing up organically in TikTok and Instagram content, with real people using it — not ads, but genuine creator posts. That kind of exposure is called UGC (explained in the glossary, but basically: authentic content made by real users, not a brand).

## Where does Campaign Hub fit in the bigger picture?

Campaign Hub doesn't work alone. It connects to four other systems:

**Notion** is where Rising Tides keeps its client relationships. When a label books a campaign, it gets logged in Notion first. Campaign Hub checks Notion to pull that booking information in automatically, so you don't have to type it twice.

**Slack** is where a special automated assistant (called Open CLAW) listens for creator booking messages and sends them to Campaign Hub for Jake to review and approve.

**Cobrand** is an external tracking tool that Rising Tides uses to monitor how the campaign posts are performing — views, likes, how many creators have submitted their videos. Campaign Hub pulls those live numbers from Cobrand and shows them right in the campaign page.

**TikTok and Instagram** are where the actual posts live. Rising Tides' own software periodically scans those platforms to find posts that match the right song, so we know which posts from which creators are actually live.

Here's the big picture in plain terms:

> Label books → Notion records it → Campaign Hub creates the campaign → creators get booked → they post on TikTok/IG → our software finds those posts → Cobrand tracks performance → Campaign Hub shows everything in one place → Jake knows exactly where things stand
