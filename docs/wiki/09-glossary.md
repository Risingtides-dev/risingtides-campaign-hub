# 9. Glossary

A plain-English definition of every term you might run into in Campaign Hub or in conversations with the team. A–Z.

---

**Active campaign**
A campaign that is still running — creators are being booked, posts are being tracked, payments haven't all been made. Shown on the Active tab of the Promotions page.

**Artist**
The musician or music act whose song the campaign is promoting. The label represents the artist and hires Rising Tides to run the promo.

**Booked**
The total amount of money committed to creator rates for a campaign. Adds up every creator's rate, paid or not. Different from **Paid**.

**Booking**
When a creator agrees (and is confirmed) to post a certain number of videos in exchange for payment. "Booking a creator" means adding them to a campaign with an agreed rate and post count.

**Budget**
The total amount of money Rising Tides has to spend on creator payments for a single campaign. Set by the label deal. Tracked in Campaign Hub.

**Campaign**
A single promotional push for one song. It has a start date, a budget, a list of creators, and a performance tracking record. Everything in Campaign Hub is organized around campaigns.

**Cobrand**
The third-party service Rising Tides uses to track how campaign posts are performing. Labels use Cobrand to see view counts, submission totals, and engagement.

**Cobrand share page**
A specific Cobrand web page for one campaign that shows live performance stats. Campaign Hub fetches data from this page once a share URL is entered.

**Cobrand share URL**
The web link to a campaign's Cobrand tracking page. Entered on the Campaign Detail page. Once set, Campaign Hub pulls live stats from it.

**CPM (Cost Per Mille)**
Cost per 1,000 views. Calculated as: total paid ÷ (total views ÷ 1,000). A lower CPM means more views per dollar spent. Used to compare campaign efficiency and creator performance.

**Creator**
An independent TikTok or Instagram user who makes videos and posts them in exchange for payment. Not an employee of Rising Tides. Sometimes called an "influencer" or "UGC creator."

**Creator Database**
The Campaign Hub page (`/creators`) listing every creator who's ever been booked on any campaign, with their stats rolled up across all campaigns.

**Creator Profile**
The page in Campaign Hub for a single creator, showing all their campaigns, posts, and payment history.

**Finished campaign**
A campaign fully wrapped — all posts delivered, all payments made. Marked with a green checkmark and shown on the Finished tab.

**Internal pages**
TikTok accounts that Rising Tides controls directly (not external creator accounts). Tracked on the Internal TikTok screen, grouped by who runs them.

**Label**
Short for "record label" — the music company that pays Rising Tides to run a UGC campaign. Examples: Warner Music Group, Atlantic Records, Republic Records. Used interchangeably with "client."

**Live Posts**
The number of TikTok or Instagram videos the scraper has matched to a campaign. Shown as a column on the Promotions list and in stats throughout the app.

**Matched video**
A TikTok or Instagram post the scraper found and confirmed belongs to a campaign — meaning it uses the right sound and was posted by a creator booked on that campaign.

**Notion**
The tool Rising Tides uses as its CRM. Where label bookings and client information are tracked. Separate from Campaign Hub.

**Notion CRM**
The specific Notion database that stores Rising Tides' client and campaign booking records. Campaign Hub pulls new entries from it.

**Open CLAW**
The automated Slack assistant that watches the company Slack for booking messages and forwards them to Campaign Hub's Slack Inbox.

**Paid**
The amount of money actually paid out to creators (versus just committed/booked). Tracked per creator and rolled up at the campaign level.

**PayPal memory**
Campaign Hub's ability to remember a creator's PayPal email from previous campaigns, so it auto-fills when that creator is added to a new one.

**Per-post rate**
The total rate divided by the number of posts owed. Example: $300 for 3 posts = $100 per post. Calculated automatically.

**Platform**
Which social media platform a campaign or creator is running on — TikTok, Instagram, or both.

**Post**
A single video uploaded by a creator to TikTok or Instagram.

**Posts done**
The number of posts the scraper has matched for a given creator on a given campaign.

**Posts owed**
The number of posts a creator agreed to make as part of their booking.

**Promotion**
What Campaign Hub calls a campaign internally — the homepage heading is "Promotions." Same thing.

**Rate**
The total payment agreed with a creator for a single campaign booking. Example: "$400 for 4 posts." The per-post rate is rate ÷ posts owed.

**Rising Tides**
The social media marketing agency this tool is built for. They run TikTok and Instagram UGC campaigns for record labels.

**Scrape Tasks**
The Campaign Hub screen (sidebar → Campaigns → Scrape Tasks) that shows the status of background scrape jobs.

**Scraping / scraper**
The automated process of searching TikTok or Instagram for videos that use a specific sound, then recording those video links and stats. See [The Scrapers](./07-scrapers.md) for the full explanation.

**Slack Inbox**
The page in Campaign Hub (`/inbox`) where booking suggestions from Open CLAW are staged for Jake's approval.

**Slug**
A short, URL-safe version of a campaign or group name. Example: "Taylor Swift - Shake It Off" → `taylor-swift-shake-it-off`. Used in Campaign Hub's web addresses (e.g. `/campaign/taylor-swift-shake-it-off`). Generated automatically from the title.

**Smaths**
Nickname for John Smathers, the lead developer. The person to ask when something in the app is broken.

**Sound**
The audio track attached to a TikTok or Instagram video. "Sounds" are how the platforms categorize music — creators choose a sound when making a video, and that sound links to all other videos using the same audio.

**Sound ID**
The unique number TikTok or Instagram assigns to a specific sound. The key the scraper uses to find all videos using that audio. Found in the sound's URL on TikTok.

**Submission (Cobrand)**
In Cobrand's terminology, a "submission" is a post link entered into Cobrand for tracking. The submission count on a campaign is how many video links have been pasted into Cobrand.

**TidesTracker**
An internal tracking record in Campaign Hub for a campaign. Used for campaign-level performance tracking over time. Created via the **Create Tracker** button on the Campaign Detail page.

**UGC (User-Generated Content)**
Videos made by real, independent social media users — not by a brand's official marketing team. Labels hire Rising Tides for UGC campaigns because creator-made content tends to outperform official ads and feel more authentic.

**Wrapped**
Informal term for a campaign that's been fully completed — all posts delivered, all creators paid. A wrapped campaign gets the green checkmark and moves to the Finished tab.
