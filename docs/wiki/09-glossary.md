# 9. Glossary

A plain-English definition of every term you might not know, A–Z.

---

**Active campaign**
A campaign that is still running — creators are being booked, posts are being tracked, payments haven't all been made yet. Shown on the Active tab of the Promotions page.

**Artist**
The musician or music act whose song the campaign is promoting. The record label represents the artist and hires Rising Tides to run the promotion.

**Booking**
When a creator agrees (and is confirmed) to post a certain number of videos in exchange for payment. "Booking a creator" means adding them to a campaign with an agreed rate and post count.

**Budget**
The total amount of money Rising Tides has to spend on creator payments for a single campaign. Set by the label deal. Tracked in Campaign Hub.

**Campaign**
A single promotional push for one song. It has a start date, a budget, a list of creators, and a performance tracking record. Everything in Campaign Hub is organized around campaigns.

**Cobrand**
The third-party service Rising Tides uses to track how campaign posts are performing. Labels use Cobrand to see view counts, submission totals, and engagement for campaigns they paid for. Campaign Hub pulls numbers from Cobrand to show in the dashboard.

**Cobrand share page**
A specific Cobrand web page for one campaign that shows its live performance stats. Campaign Hub fetches data from this page automatically when a share URL is entered.

**Cobrand share URL**
The web link to a campaign's Cobrand tracking page. Entered on the Campaign Detail page in Campaign Hub. Once set, Campaign Hub pulls live stats from it.

**Content type**
The style of video a creator is expected to make. Examples: "dance," "lip sync," "POV," "voiceover." Some campaigns specify the types they want.

**CPM (Cost Per Mille)**
Cost per 1,000 views. Calculated as: total paid ÷ (total views ÷ 1,000). A lower CPM means you're getting more views per dollar spent. Used to compare campaign efficiency and creator performance.

**Creator**
An independent TikTok or Instagram user who makes videos and posts them in exchange for payment. Not an employee of Rising Tides. Also called an "influencer" or "UGC creator."

**Creator Database**
The page in Campaign Hub (`/creators`) that lists every creator who has ever been booked on any campaign, with their stats across all campaigns.

**Creator profile**
The page in Campaign Hub for a single creator, showing all their campaigns, posts, and payment history.

**Finished campaign**
A campaign that has been fully wrapped — all posts delivered, payments made. Marked with a green checkmark and moved to the Finished tab.

**Label**
Short for "record label" — the music company that pays Rising Tides to run a UGC campaign. Examples: Warner Music Group, Atlantic Records, Republic Records. Also referred to as the "client."

**Matched video**
A TikTok or Instagram post that the scraper found and matched to a campaign — meaning it uses the right sound and was posted by a creator booked on that campaign.

**Notion**
The tool Rising Tides uses as a client relationship manager (CRM). Where label bookings and client information are tracked. Separate from Campaign Hub.

**Notion CRM**
The specific Notion database that stores Rising Tides' client and campaign booking records. Campaign Hub can pull new entries from it automatically.

**Open CLAW**
The automated Slack assistant that monitors booking messages in Slack and forwards them to Campaign Hub's Slack Inbox as booking suggestions.

**PayPal memory**
Campaign Hub's ability to remember a creator's PayPal email from previous campaigns, so it auto-fills when that creator is added to a new campaign.

**Per-post rate**
The payment amount divided by the number of posts owed. Example: $300 for 3 posts = $100 per post. Calculated automatically by Campaign Hub.

**Platform**
Which social media platform a creator will post on — TikTok, Instagram, or both.

**Post**
A single video uploaded by a creator to TikTok or Instagram.

**Posts done**
The number of posts that have been found (matched) by the scraper for a given creator on a given campaign.

**Posts owed**
The number of posts a creator agreed to make as part of their booking.

**Promotion**
What Campaign Hub calls a campaign. The homepage heading says "Promotions." Same thing.

**Rate**
The total payment agreed with a creator for a campaign booking. Example: "$400 for 4 posts." The per-post rate is the rate divided by posts owed.

**Rising Tides**
The social media marketing agency this tool is built for. They manage TikTok and Instagram UGC campaigns for record labels.

**Scraping / scraper**
The automated process of searching TikTok or Instagram for videos that use a specific sound, then recording those video links and stats. See [The Scrapers](./07-scrapers.md) for a full explanation.

**Slack inbox**
The page in Campaign Hub (`/inbox`) where booking suggestions from Open CLAW (the Slack bot) are staged for Jake's approval.

**Slug**
A short, URL-safe version of a campaign name. Example: "Taylor Swift - Shake It Off" becomes `taylor-swift-shake-it-off`. Used in Campaign Hub's web addresses (e.g. `/campaign/taylor-swift-shake-it-off`). Generated automatically from the campaign title.

**Sound**
The audio track attached to a TikTok or Instagram video. "Sounds" are how the platforms categorize music — creators choose a sound when making a video, and that sound links to all other videos using the same audio.

**Sound ID**
The unique number TikTok or Instagram assigns to a specific sound. It's the key that the scraper uses to find all videos using that audio track. Example: `7123456789012345678`. Found in the sound's URL on TikTok.

**Submission (Cobrand)**
In Cobrand's terminology, a "submission" is a post link that has been submitted to Cobrand for tracking. The submission count on a campaign is how many video links have been entered into Cobrand.

**TidesTracker**
An internal tracking record created in Campaign Hub for a campaign. Used to monitor campaign-level performance over time. Created via the "Create Tracker" button on the Campaign Detail page.

**UGC (User Generated Content)**
Videos made by real, independent social media users — not by a brand's official marketing team. Labels hire Rising Tides to arrange UGC campaigns because creator-made content tends to perform better and feel more authentic than official ads.

**Wrapped**
Informal term for a campaign that's been fully completed — all posts delivered, all creators paid. A wrapped campaign gets the green checkmark and moves to the Finished tab.
