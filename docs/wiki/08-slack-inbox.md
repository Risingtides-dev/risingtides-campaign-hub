# 8. The Slack Inbox

**Where:** Click "Slack Inbox" in the sidebar under Intake.

**Who uses it:** Jake, to approve or dismiss creator booking recommendations that come in from Slack.

---

## What It Is

The Slack Inbox is a holding area for booking recommendations. Here's how it works:

1. Someone posts a booking in the Rising Tides Slack channel — something like: "Book @xyzbca_quote for 5 posts at $150 on Sombr Homewrecker."
2. An AI assistant called Open CLAW reads that message and translates it into a structured booking: creator username, rate, number of posts, which campaign it's for.
3. Open CLAW sends the booking to Campaign Hub, where it appears in the Slack Inbox as a **Pending** item.
4. Jake reviews it here and clicks **Approve** or **Dismiss**.

Approving a booking automatically adds the creator(s) to the campaign. No manual data entry needed.

---

## What You See

The page header shows the page title and the count of pending items.

Items are grouped into three sections, stacked top to bottom:

### Pending Approval

These are new bookings that haven't been acted on yet. Each card shows:

- The original Slack message (so you can see exactly what was written)
- Which campaign the booking is for (sometimes Open CLAW suggests a campaign; sometimes you pick one from a dropdown)
- Each creator being booked: username, posts owed, rate
- An **Approve** button and a **Dismiss** button

Clicking **Approve** adds all the creators to the campaign immediately and moves the card to the "Recently Approved" section.

Clicking **Dismiss** removes the card from the pending list and moves it to "Dismissed."

### Recently Approved

The last 10 bookings you approved. Shown for reference, no action needed.

### Dismissed

The last 5 bookings you dismissed. Also shown for reference.

---

## If the Inbox Is Empty

If no bookings have come in from Slack yet, the page shows a friendly empty state with a note explaining how Open CLAW works. This is normal — the inbox only fills up when new Slack bookings are parsed.

---

## What to Do If a Booking Looks Wrong

If Open CLAW got the campaign wrong or the rate is off, you have two options:

1. **Dismiss the booking** and add the creator manually from the campaign detail page.
2. **Approve the booking** and then edit the creator's details in the campaign creator table afterward.

The approval just adds the creator — you can always edit them afterwards.
