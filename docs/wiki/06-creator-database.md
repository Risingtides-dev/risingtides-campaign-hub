# 6. The Creator Database

**Where:** Click "Creator Database" in the sidebar under the Creators section.

**Who uses it:** Jake and account managers when they want to see who's in the network, check a creator's track record, or find someone to book for a new campaign.

---

## What It Is

The Creator Database is a roster of every creator who has ever been booked on any Rising Tides campaign. It pulls together information from all campaigns — so if a creator has been on five different campaigns, their total earnings, total posts, and total views are all combined into a single row.

This is different from the creator table inside a single campaign. That shows one creator's role on one campaign. This page shows every creator's aggregate history across all campaigns.

---

## What You See

A search bar at the top, and below it a sortable table.

**Search:** Type any username to filter the table instantly. Clear the box to see everyone again.

**Table columns:**

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Creator** | Their username, with a TikTok icon. Click to go to their profile page. |
| **Campaigns** | How many campaigns they've been booked on |
| **Posts Owed** | Total posts owed across all campaigns |
| **Posts Done** | Total posts completed across all campaigns |
| **Total Earned** | Sum of all rates paid (or agreed to) across all campaigns |
| **Total Payout** | Amount actually paid out |
| **Total Views** | Combined views across all their matched posts |
| **Avg CPM** | Their average cost per thousand views — useful for gauging value |
| **Platform** | Which platform(s) they work on |

Every column header is clickable — click once to sort ascending, again to sort descending.

---

## What It's Good For

- **Finding creators to book again** — sort by "Avg CPM" to find who delivers the most views per dollar spent
- **Checking if a creator has worked with us before** — search their username before reaching out
- **Spotting creators who consistently under-deliver** — sort by Posts Done vs. Posts Owed

---

## Clicking Into a Creator

Click any row to open that creator's full profile page.
